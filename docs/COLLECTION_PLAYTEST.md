# Human collection acceptance

Engineering rehearsal is not a human game. M30 remains open until the following evidence exists.

## Ready to use

Choose a universe in the collection, open its preview, then Play Today. Name the game, review generation choices, create it and open the GM table. The table starts before any players. Invite friends using the code/link from the player panel. Players choose a compatible saved Character or create one. Scenes, map, private notebook, checks and player removal are available at the table.

## Three contrasting sessions

1. Classic Fantasy or Space Opera: run a complete rescue/defense route, including its final obligations and rewards.
2. Cosmic Investigation or Gothic Horror: investigate evidence, use pressure resources, make a consequential choice and finish the aftermath.
3. Age of Sail or Mecha & Kaiju: resolve a transport/rescue problem with limited capacity, an alternative route and a recorded cost.

Use at least two friends, one GM and different devices. Play from opening to aftermath. Include a late join, a player reload/network interruption, one GM recovery, a removed participant, a real requested dice check, inventory/reward and progression. Do not replace these games with automated API calls.

## Record evidence

For each game record date, universe/Pack version, Kit, participant count, approximate duration, which choices actually mattered, where the GM searched for controls, whether players understood their sheet, recovery problems, unreadable layouts and content contradictions. Avoid collecting credentials or private player data. Mark each issue as reproducible, editorial or preference. Fix reproducible failures and rerun the relevant human step.

For each non-English content locale, a native reviewer must review names, adventure instructions and rules terminology. Current adventure content uses declared English fallback. UI language selection alone does not satisfy translation acceptance. Keyboard/assistive-technology users must review the table and creation flow; the narrow automated/375px checks are only engineering evidence.

The owner can accept the open-source product when actual full games work for the intended group and recorded blockers are resolved. Until then M30 product acceptance is open.
